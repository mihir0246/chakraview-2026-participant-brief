00 // The Council's Mandate

What is CHAKRAVIEW?

CHAKRAVIEW is a month-long, high-intensity executive simulation engineered to test, develop and evaluate leadership competency among club members. The initiative creates a parallel operating council in which participants must plan, coordinate, execute, document and defend real initiatives across community impact, campus engagement and fiscal operations.

The Idea Behind the Name

ElementMeaning in Chakraview
ChakraA connected wheel: every part contributes to the movement of the whole.
ViewParticipants experience the club from a different operational perspective.
CouncilLeadership is treated as a collective system of responsibilities, not a one-person hierarchy.
OfficeEach participant is accountable for one portfolio while remaining responsible for the success of the full council.

How the Event Will Turn Out

  • 60 active Rotaractors are divided into 6 balanced Councils of exactly 10 members each.
  • Each Council contains 10 Sabhasad offices. The role names are deliberately non-hierarchical so that the simulation does not frame one participant as 'above' another.
  • Each Council receives Avenue 1 and Avenue 2. These two avenues become the Council's principal project battlefield for the month.
  • Participants work through a fixed campaign calendar: Council assembly, Sankalpa (PoA), first execution phase, academic blackout, flagship execution, Chronicle and audit, and the Raj Sabha defence.
  • Individual Sabhasad offices carry measurable deliverables—documentation, finance, people, protocol, operations and strategy—while avenue leads carry the project requirements.
  • Cross-council attendance and mobilization create additional points, while reporting, financial and academic compliance failures trigger deductions.

THE TEN SABHASADS

Sabhasad — Nayak

Primary Mandate: The visionary and chief representative of the Council. Responsible for setting the strategic roadmap (PoA), aligning the team's agenda, and ensuring overall direction and momentum.

Scoring Basis: PoA + 2 formal meetings

Sabhasad — Lekhak

Primary Mandate: The master of records and governance. Tasked with meticulously documenting all proceedings, drafting the formal Minutes of Meetings (MoMs), and compiling compliance dossiers and PDRs.

Scoring Basis: MoMs + PDRs

Sabhasad — Senapati

Primary Mandate: The operations chief. Coordinates the 4 Karta Sabhasads, drives internal synergy, manages the delegation of tasks, and actively resolves any crises or roadblocks that arise.

Scoring Basis: Delegation dossier + crisis report

Sabhasad — Bhandari

Primary Mandate: The guardian of the treasury. Manages the council's budget, conceptualizes and executes micro-fundraisers, and ensures absolute financial transparency through audited ledgers.

Scoring Basis: Fundraiser + audited ledger + bonuses

Sabhasad — Saarthi

Primary Mandate: The driver of people and engagement. Focuses on maintaining high attendance, empowering GBMs with active roles, resolving internal conflicts, and building team morale.

Scoring Basis: Muster roll + GBM delegation

Sabhasad — Pahredar

Primary Mandate: The keeper of order and protocol. Ensures meeting decorum, strict punctuality, quorum fulfillment, and conducts thorough venue and technical readiness checks before events.

Scoring Basis: SAA/protocol + logistics checks

Sabhasad — Karta (x4)

Primary Mandate: The core execution force of the Council. These 4 offices share the responsibility of bringing the assigned Avenue deliverables to life, handling both ground initiatives and digital requirements.

Scoring Basis: Avenue 1 & Avenue 2 scores

04 // Avenue Deliverables Docket

Council 1

Avenue 1: Community Service (Max 1000)
• Flagship Beneficiary Ground Drive (600 pts): Conceptualization and physical execution of a welfare initiative (donation, animal welfare, environmental clean-up or healthcare camp).
• Beneficiary Reach Threshold (200 pts): Verified direct positive impact on min 30 distinct beneficiaries, with geolocated evidence or recipient-organization sign-offs.
• Pre-Execution Logistics & Safety Audit (200 pts): Advance risk assessment, emergency protocols and volunteer safety measures.

Avenue 2: Editorial Service (Max 1000)
• Official Mock Newsletter / E-Bulletin (500 pts): Min 4 formatted pages documenting the tenure, member spotlights, campus write-ups and project recaps.
• Thematic Long-Form Article / Op-Ed (300 pts): Original, research-backed think piece or campus commentary; min 800 words.
• Linguistic Proofing & Copy Clearance (200 pts): Audit grammar, tone and formatting across public broadcasts, captions and letters.

Council 2

Avenue 1: Professional Development (Max 1000)
• Expert Speaker Session / Technical Workshop (700 Offline / 450 Online): Career-enhancement workshop led by an external professional, startup founder or notable alumnus.
• Attendance & Engagement Benchmark (200 pts): Verified live audience of ≥30 active students throughout the session.
• Takeaway Resource Kit & Summary Deck (100 pts): Standardized toolkit, cheatsheet or guide distributed within 24 hours post-session.

Avenue 2: Digital Communication (Max 1000)
• Unified Brand Asset Package (400 pts): Standardized digital design system (posters, banners, speaker flyers, certificates).
• Interactive Digital Tool Deployment (350 pts): Functional interactive tool (automated registration, Kahoot/Mentimeter or mini-site).
• Central Cloud Archive Management (250 pts): Updated Google Drive repository for raw media, final creatives, vector assets and official files.

Council 3

Avenue 1: Sports (Max 1000)
• Mini-Tournament / Fixture Execution (700 pts): Organized competitive knockout event with verified scorekeeping and formal brackets.
• Athlete Roster Threshold (200 pts): Minimum 16 registered competitors/teams with active participation.
• Match Safety & Equipment Checklist (100 pts): Certified officials/referees, adequate equipment and operational first-aid station.

Avenue 2: Social Media Relations (Max 1000)
• Multi-Phase Themed Awareness Campaign (500 pts): Min 3 coordinated feed posts plus daily interactive stories (polls, Q&As, countdowns).
• Short-Form Trend Reel Production (300 pts): Script, direct, edit and post a theme-relevant branded reel using suitable trend elements.
• Data Analytics & Reach Dossier (200 pts): Post-campaign audit covering reach, impressions, profile activity, link clicks and demographics.

Council 4

Avenue 1: Entrepreneurship Development (Max 1000)
• Shark Tank / Business Pitch Challenge (700 pts): Intra-college micro-pitch competition or corporate case-study challenge.
• External Business Mentor / Jury Outreach (200 pts): Pitch, confirm and onboard an entrepreneur, student founder or professional as judge/mentor.
• Evaluation Framework & Rubric Design (100 pts): Objective scoring matrix across market viability, unit economics, scaling strategy and innovation.

Avenue 2: Public Relations (Max 1000)
• External Non-Club Student Inflow (500 pts): Minimum 10 verified external, non-Rotaract college students at the Council's flagship events.
• Institutional Press Release & Media Copy (300 pts): Formal publication-ready press release for campus publications or institutional bulletin channels.
• Multi-Channel Distribution Blast Grid (200 pts): Promotional broadcasts across ≥10 distinct institutional, department or student groups.

Council 5

Avenue 1: Shiksha (Max 1000)
• Interactive Educational Drive (700 pts): Teaching or skill-development workshop for underprivileged children or junior school batches.
• Curriculum Design & Stationery Distribution (200 pts): Age-appropriate lesson syllabus and individual learning kits for attendees.
• Learning Retention Assessment (100 pts): Exit test, verbal recap or quiz validating concept comprehension.

Avenue 2: International Service (Max 1000)
• Twin Club Letter of Agreement (500 pts): Formal diplomatic liaison with out-of-district/state club; draft, exchange and sign bilateral agreement.
• Cross-District Virtual Fellowship (350 pts): Joint online meeting/cultural exchange sharing organizational practices, city culture and regional insights.
• Global Observance Digital Initiative (150 pts): Joint digital creative or statement marking an officially recognized UN/international observance day.

Council 6

Avenue 1: Club Service (Max 1000)
• Flagship Intra-Club Fellowship Event (700 pts): Offline bonding experience integrating GBMs with executive leadership.
• Quorum & Participation Threshold (200 pts): Verified physical attendance of ≥25 participants throughout the program.
• Detailed Run-of-Show Protocol (100 pts): Minute-by-minute schedule with emcee scripts, buffers, rules and host management.

Avenue 2: Marketing (Max 1000)
• Club Merchandise Strategy & Unit Economics (500 pts): Complete club merchandise proposal with mockups, vendor quotes, pricing slabs and estimated margins.
• Commercial Sponsorship Deck (350 pts): Corporate sponsorship brochure with tiered ROI slabs (Title, Powered By, Associate).
• Lead Conversion & Acquisition Funnel (150 pts): Active spreadsheet tracking leads, clicks, confirmed registrations and ground conversions.

13 // The Council Charter

  • Own the Office: Your Sabhasad belongs to you. Track its deadlines, evidence and outcomes.
  • Serve the Council: A successful individual portfolio does not compensate for a failed Council.
  • Document the Work: No claim should depend on memory alone; preserve the evidence.
  • Respect the Calendar: The academic blackout is absolute.
  • Account for Every Rupee: Financial transparency is a scored responsibility, not an afterthought.
  • Develop Others: Use the simulation to give active GBMs meaningful ownership and speaking roles.
  • Cross the Walls: Support other Councils and build legitimate synergy points.
  • Defend the Chronicle: Every Council must be able to explain what it promised, what it delivered, what changed and why.
